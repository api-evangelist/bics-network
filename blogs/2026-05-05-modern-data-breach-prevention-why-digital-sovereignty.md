Amid intensifying geopolitical outbreaks, the global digital frontlines are under attack. From Border Gateway Protocol (or BGP) hijacking to data breaches, the digital sovereignty of countries and institutions stands threatened. Recent data breaches reveal the scale of risk: More than 19 million records of personal/confidential data of French citizen have been stolen.
